# Team responsibilities

“Project Owner” represents the requesting team member until their name is supplied. These are proposed lead assignments.

| Member | Lead roles | Responsibilities | Reviewer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Project Owner | Setup; writing | Workstation/environment setup, repository organization, meeting notes, milestone coordination, final report and presentation integration | Jeff reviews setup; Avaneesh reviews technical claims |
| Jeff | Software; networking | Data ingestion and validation, Python pipeline, reproducible execution, optional Unity replay, authorized downloads and transfer integrity, collaboration access workflow | Avaneesh reviews pipeline; Project Owner reviews reproducibility |
| Avaneesh | Research; algorithm design | Literature matrix, dataset selection, threat model, features, models, split design, metrics, privacy–utility experiment design | Jeff reviews implementation feasibility; Project Owner reviews scope |

All three contribute to experiments, interpret findings, and review the final report. A lead owns coordination and acceptance of their area rather than doing every task alone.

## Working cadence
Hold one weekly milestone review, maintain task issues with an owner and acceptance criterion, and use pull requests for method or implementation changes. Record dataset access decisions and experiment configurations. Freeze evaluation choices before final test runs. Raise access or feasibility blockers by the end of week 2.

Networking covers secure transfer, source verification, and team collaboration. A multiplayer or live streaming system is outside this proposal.
