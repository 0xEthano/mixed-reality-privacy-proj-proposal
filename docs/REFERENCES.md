# References

Reviewed 10.6.2026

## Required literature

**R1.** Diana Romero, Yasra Chandio, Fatima Anwar, and Salma Elmalaki. *MURMR: A Multimodal Sensing Framework for Automated Group Behavior Analysis in Mixed Reality.* arXiv:2507.11797, revision v3, March 31, 2026. [2026 revision](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.11797v3). Initial preprint appeared in 2025; the requested 2026 year refers here to the revised version. Relevance: structural and temporal group behavior analysis.

**R2.** Ismat Jarin, Yu Duan, Rahmadi Trimananda, Hao Cui, Salma Elmalaki, and Athina Markopoulou. *BehaVR: User Identification Based on VR Sensor Data.* 2023. [arXiv:2308.07304](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.07304). Relevance: sensor-based identification and app/device adversary models.

**R3.** Jonathan Liebers et al. *Understanding User Identification in Virtual Reality Through Behavioral Biometrics and the Effect of Body Normalization.* CHI 2021. [DOI:10.1145/3411764.3445528](https://doi.org/10.1145/3411764.3445528). [Author institution and dataset links](https://hci.informatik.uni-due.de/publikationen/understanding-user-identification-in-virtual-reality-through-behavioral-biometrics-and-the-effect-of-body-normalization). Relevance: behavioral biometrics and normalization experiments.

**R4.** Mark Roman Miller, Fernanda Herrera, Hanseul Jun, James A. Landay, and Jeremy N. Bailenson. *Personal identifiability of user tracking data during observation of 360-degree VR video.* Scientific Reports 10, 17404, 2020. [DOI:10.1038/s41598-020-74486-y](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-020-74486-y). Relevance: identifiability from ordinary viewing-related tracking data.

## Additional leads

[DOI:10.1109/TIFS.2020.3036218](https://doi.org/10.1109/TIFS.2020.3036218). [Official SCUT-DHGA repository and access instructions](https://github.com/SCUT-BIP-Lab/SCUT-DHGA). Candidate gesture dataset with a separate release agreement.
